Engineering Excellence

Engineered for the podium, stage & boardroom

Works behind a locked screen (Pocket Mode)

A lightweight, background MediaSession service intercepts physical volume button clicks even when your phone is locked and sleeping in your blazer. Volume audio resets silently with zero rings or alerts.

3D Gyro Laser Pointer & Spotlight

Point your phone at the screen like a physical wand. Gyroscope sensors render an ultra-realistic glowing red laser dot or dim the background into a crisp spotlight focus cutout over any app.

Smart Touchpad Fallback

Phone without a physical gyroscope sensor (e.g. OPPO A78 5G and budget smartphones)? NXTslide automatically falls back to a silky-smooth touchpad surface for precision laser pointing.

Multi-Presenter Synchronization

Team presentation, debate, or panel discussion? Up to 5 mobile phones can connect to the same slide deck simultaneously using the 6-letter room code with live client status monitoring.

Sub-2ms Native C# SendInput

Native Win32 key injection compiled in C# (`keySender.exe`). Unlike laggy browser-based simulated keystrokes, slides change instantly the microsecond you press the volume key.

Zero-Install Portable Mode

Presenting from a locked enterprise podium laptop without administrator rights? Download `NXTslide-Portable.exe` to a USB drive and double-click. Zero installation, zero registry pollution.

Global Cloud Relay (Any Network)

Presenter PC on university Wi-Fi, phone on 5G/LTE? The global WebSocket relay effortlessly tunnels through campus NATs, corporate firewalls, and guest portals with sub-100ms response.

Universal Software Profiles

Instant one-click mappings for Microsoft PowerPoint, Google Slides, Apple Keynote, Canva Presentations, Adobe Acrobat PDF, Web PDF Readers, LibreOffice Impress, and Prezi.

✦ The Origin Story · Why I Built NXTslide
"It started with a ruined college presentation."

"I spent an entire night preparing for a major college presentation. I skipped sleep, rehearsed every single line, and timed my slides down to the second. By morning, I knew the material inside out. Because practically no college student can afford an expensive ₹5,000+ Logitech remote, I did what almost everyone does: I asked a classmate to sit by the laptop and advance the slides for me."

"My presentation started, and everything was flowing naturally—until the first mistake. I needed Slide 3; he accidentally jumped to Slide 5. I said 'previous slide, please', and he panicked and opened the wrong page. When I asked for Slide 7, he went back to Slide 3. In less than 30 seconds, my rhythm was shattered, the room was distracted, and days of hard work and preparation felt wasted by someone else's missed clicks."

"Walking off that stage, one realization hit me: If I did all the hard work, why should control of my presentation be in someone else's hands?"

"As an engineering student, I looked around. Students and teachers can't afford expensive physical remotes or monthly subscription software. But there is one device every single student and speaker has in their hand 24/7: their smartphone. That day, I set out to build NXTslide so that no presenter ever has to say 'next slide please' again."

Dilpreet Singh Verma
Student Engineer & Creator of NXTslide · EventFold Studio
