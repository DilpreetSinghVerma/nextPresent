Why NXTslide

Built for the stage, not the screen

Works behind a locked screen

A high-priority background audio session keeps the service alive. Lock your phone, pocket it, and present for hours. Less than 1% battery per hour.

Sub-2ms keystroke injection

Native Windows keystroke injection via a lightweight C# helper. No perceptible delay between click and slide change.

Cloud relay — any network

Phone on cellular, laptop on hotel Wi-Fi? The cloud relay bridges them instantly with a 6-letter room code.

Zero-install portable

No admin rights needed. Run the portable .exe from a USB drive on a locked corporate laptop.

Completely silent

Volume resets silently. No chime. No notification. Your audience hears nothing except your voice.

Universal software support

Switch profiles in one click — PowerPoint, Google Slides, Keynote, Canva, PDF viewers, Prezi. Each profile sends the correct keystrokes automatically.
