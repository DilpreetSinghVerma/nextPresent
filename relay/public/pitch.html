The Market Pain

Presenting Today is Broken, Clunky & Expensive

Presenters face massive friction before they even speak their first word.

01
$100 Hardware Trap
Dedicated clickers (Logitech Spotlight, etc.) cost $50 to $130+. They require USB-A dongles useless on modern USB-C laptops, easily get lost, and die mid-talk.
02
Firewall & Wi-Fi Failures
Legacy mobile clicker apps require exact same-subnet Wi-Fi connections that are routinely blocked by university, corporate, and hotel firewalls (client isolation).
03
Fragmented Audience Tools
85% of presentations suffer from audience disengagement. Tools like Slido charge $15–$35/month, forcing speakers to juggle multiple screens and browser tabs.
The Innovation

NXTslide: A Unified Presentation Superpower

Zero hardware. Zero network configuration. Complete presentation command in your pocket.

Instant QR / Room Code Pairing
Scan a QR code or enter a 4-letter room code on any phone. Connects in under 2 seconds without configuring IP addresses or Bluetooth pairing.
Universal Cloud Relay (<50ms)
Engineered on WebSocket cloud infrastructure. Works effortlessly across cellular data (4G/5G), office Wi-Fi, and mobile hotspots, bypassing local firewalls.
Digital Gyro Laser & Spotlight
Transforms the smartphone's internal motion sensors into a real-time virtual laser pointer and spotlight beam on any projector screen.
Pocket Teleprompter & Notes
Discreetly displays speaker notes, slide elapsed timer, and haptic vibration pacing cues right on the phone in your hand.
